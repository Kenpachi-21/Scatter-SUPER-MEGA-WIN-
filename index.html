Which license are you applying for?

Every license has its own exam and reviewer. Start with the one that matches where you are today.

SP

Student Permit

The first step for new drivers. Covers the written exam on basic traffic rules and road signs.

Minimum age
16
NP

Non-Professional License

For private driving. Includes the written exam and a practical driving test.

Minimum age
17
PRO

Professional License

For those who drive for a living. Builds on the non-professional exam with added requirements.

Minimum age
18

Requirements and fees are set by the LTO and can change. Confirm the latest details with the LTO before you apply.
